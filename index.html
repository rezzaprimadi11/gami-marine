From first note to order

A straightforward buying sequence.

We keep the steps in order: availability first, sample next, order only when the terms are clear.

  1. 01↗

    Send the buying brief

    Species, form, quantity, destination and documents needed. The more specific the brief, the quicker we can check it.

  2. 02↗

    We check real availability

    We confirm the current specification with our supplier and approved processing partner, then share actual-goods photos or video.

  3. 03↗

    Review a paid sample

    Samples are arranged for your evaluation before an order is placed. Sample terms are confirmed directly.

  4. 04↗

    Confirm in writing

    Order details follow only after written confirmation and a proforma invoice with the agreed deposit terms.
