From your spec to a sealed box

One path for every shipment, so you know what happens and when.

  1. Send your spec

    Species, size grade, format, packing and destination port.

  2. Match supply

    We source from farms and landing points in West Kalimantan and confirm availability.

  3. Grade and pack

    Sorted, iced or frozen, then sealed in insulated boxes or cartons.

  4. Load and ship

    Palletised into the reefer, documents prepared for your port.

Tell us what you need to land.

Send the species, size and volume you buy. We reply with availability and a packing proposal.
